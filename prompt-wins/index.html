What a working prompt actually contains

A prompt that produces usable work — not generic filler — has five parts. PromptPack Pro's packs are built this way, and you can judge any library by the same frame:

  • Role: who the AI should write as — your trade, your tone, your customer.
  • Job: one clear output — a quote, a follow-up email, a product description — never "help me with marketing."
  • Inputs: the facts to slot in — names, prices, dates — marked so you fill them in seconds.
  • Shape: length, format and structure of the answer you want back.
  • Checks: what to fix before you send it — numbers, names, promises you cannot keep.

The fastest way to learn the frame is to study prompts that already have it. A structured course-style ebook walks through prompt engineering end to end — role, inputs, iteration — instead of handing you a pile of disconnected one-liners.
