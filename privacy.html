Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

Free teaser. Nothing. The 5 free prompts load with no email, no account, no tracking beyond basic anonymous rate limiting.

Purchases. Your email address (to deliver your pack and receipt), your chosen profession, and payment metadata from Stripe. We never see or store your card details — Stripe handles payment directly.

What we do with it

Your email is used to deliver your pack, send your receipt, and contact you about your order. We never sell data, never share it with third parties for marketing, and never use your business in our marketing without your permission.

AI generation

Your profession choice is sent to our AI provider (Cloudflare Workers AI) solely to generate your pack. Inputs are not used for training and are not retained beyond production.

Data deletion

Want your data deleted? Email info@mehyar.us from the address you purchased with and we'll delete your order record and pack within 30 days. Note: deleting your order record disables your private pack link.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@mehyar.us.
